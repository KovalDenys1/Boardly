kept in kenney/LICENSE-kenney-CC0.txt. Thank you, Kenney.

Each file is the pack's sample of the same name, mixed down to mono, trimmed, peak-normalised
to -1 dBFS and encoded as MP3 (44.1 kHz, 96 kbps CBR).
