Kenney sound set (public/sounds/kenney/)

All 60 files come from four Kenney packs, downloaded from https://kenney.nl on 2026-10-18.
Every pack is released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
"You may use these assets in personal and commercial projects. Credit (Kenney or
www.kenney.nl) would be nice but is not mandatory." The licence text shipped with the packs is
kept beside the files as LICENSE-kenney-CC0.txt. Thank you, Kenney.

Each file is the pack's sample of the same name, mixed down to mono, trimmed, peak-normalised
to -1 dBFS and encoded as MP3 (44.1 kHz, 96 kbps CBR).
